Microscopy images into numbers you can check

rauntruth.com is a free research tool for neuroscientists. Open a fluorescence-microscopy image and a study finds, counts and measures what it is built for, then lets you review every result and correct it.

Open the app

What it does

Each analysis on the site is a study: a viewer and an algorithm for one question about one kind of image. A study proposes every candidate it finds, says why it kept or rejected each one, and reports counts, densities and lengths for the whole image or a region you draw. You keep the final say: accept, reject or correct any result, and export the numbers as CSV.

Your corrections can be sent back as ground truth, which is what the algorithms are tested and improved against, in the open.

Studies

    Your images stay with you

    Everything runs in your browser. Images are analysed on your own computer, from a file or a shared Google Drive folder, and are never uploaded to a server; the site has none. Once opened, the app works offline too. Signing in with Google is optional: it carries your settings between devices, and lets a study's data managers work on that study's Google Drive folders. The privacy policy has the details.
