Filters

Instant, and remembered in this browser across images and visits. Untick a filter to switch it off. Click a marking on the image to add or remove it by hand. Starred (★) NoRs stay in the list whatever the filters say; removed ones stay greyed and are left out of the totals. Stars and removals are remembered per file and finder.
