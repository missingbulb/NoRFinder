# nor-study — the NoR Finder study's own pack

The home for what is specific to the NoR Finder study, `studies/nor/`: the skill that improves its
finders on new ground truth, and the task that takes in the ground truth people submit from its
page. A study is removable, so nothing outside `studies/nor/` and this pack depends on it; this
pack may depend on the repo's own `nor-finder` pack, never the reverse.

A lesson that holds for the repo, the shell or another study goes in `nor-finder`; one that would
hold in another repo goes to the Claudinite canon.
